# Changes

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-10)

This update adds tools for running multiple calculations locally, keeping long calculations going and visualizing properties calculated by NWChem, as well as reaction-path calculations (NEB and chains of calculations), preparing metal complexes (xTB), exporting figures and videos, getting structures (small-molecule CIF, PubChem) and building them (polydentate and η ligands, solvation shells).

### Before using these features

- The queue runs sequentially while MolForma is open. What goes on after the window closes is the one running calculation; the waiting jobs are restored at the next startup but start nothing until you resume the queue. Concurrent jobs are unsupported. Whether a calculation survives a restart of the computer or a logout depends on the operating system’s settings.
- Remote runs are for computers that accept a connection without a password (a key usable through `ssh-agent` or `~/.ssh/config`). Typed passwords, one-time passwords and accepting host keys are not supported, and additional environment variables are not passed to the destination. Shell submission was checked over real SSH on Linux; Slurm and PBS were checked with test stand-ins only. The default headers are for one node.
- “Prepare a new calculation” prepares a new job from the displayed geometry. To carry over NWChem’s database or a Hessian, use “Prepare to continue” or “Next calculation from this result” in “History”. A restart copies the database of the original job into a new job folder and begins with `restart`. Coordinate scans, IRC and BSSE-corrected calculations, and calculations whose `permanent_dir` or `scratch_dir` is outside the job, cannot be restarted, and a restarted calculation cannot change the molecule or the constraints.
- NMR J splitting and paramagnetic NMR, resonance Raman and powder/high-spin ESR are unsupported. ECD is not converted to Δε, nor ORD β to specific rotation.
- For free-energy comparisons, check correction temperatures, sources and imaginary frequencies. The thermochemistry corrections replace only the vibrational parts of the values NWChem printed; the defaults (scale 1, harmonic oscillators, 1 atm) keep NWChem’s values. Rate constants use the Eyring equation with a transmission coefficient of 1, taking the free-energy difference from the reference stage as the barrier. Conformer populations describe only the selected set, with degeneracy 1 per group.
- Bond indices depend on the basis set and can be unreliable with diffuse functions. The orbital weights of an excited state come only from the contributions NWChem printed (amplitudes above 0.05 in magnitude).
- Check CUBE units against the generating calculation. A color grid must have matching atom order and coordinates and cover the entire surface; it is not aligned automatically.
- NEB cannot be combined with fixed atoms or ghosts, TDDFT, property calculations, the follow-up single point, the BSSE correction, CUBE or MOLDEN output, or a restart. The end must have the same atoms in the same order as the start. NWChem's NEB ends normally also when it stops at its maximum steps, and MolForma does not treat that as a failure (the result viewer shows it as not converged). The highest point estimates the transition state; confirm it with a transition-state search and frequencies.
- A chained calculation takes in its structure and saves its input bundle when its source has finished normally. Only the structure is carried over, not molecular orbitals or Hessians.
- xtb is not shipped with MolForma (LGPL-3.0): install it and set it as “xTB executable” in “Edit → Preferences”. External point charges are left out, and ghosts and elements heavier than Rn cannot be handled. It prepares structures with a semiempirical method and does not replace a DFT optimization.
- Small-molecule CIF files are read as isolated molecules; periodic calculations (NWChem's `system crystal`) are not supported. The unit cell may hold up to 6,000 atoms, and by default only the major disorder component is read. For CIF files without hydrogens, check the formal charges before adding them.
- When a name matches several compounds in PubChem, the first one is opened. Compounds with a metal and without a 3D structure (PubChem records most complexes as separate ions) are not opened; use the coordination builder or a crystal structure.
- Polydentate ligands keep the shape of their part, so the center–donor distances may not suit the metal. Sets of sites more than 25° off the donor directions are refused. Refine with xTB or the like before a calculation.
- Solvation shells are a simple placement of rigid solvent molecules at contact distances; they do not reproduce hydrogen-bond networks or the solvent's density, and no periodic solvent box is made.
- MP4 export needs `ffmpeg` (with an H.264 encoder) in PATH. GIF frames have at most 255 colors each, and edges on a transparent background are not smoothed. SVG is offered by builds with the Qt SVG module.

### Saving and compatibility

Existing MolForma projects (`molforma-project`, format version 1) remain readable and writable. New calculation settings, the restart settings (`document.nwchem.restart`) the kind of job script (`document.nwchem_execution.job_script`, `job_queue`) and the NEB settings and end (`document.nwchem.neb_*`; the end as coordinates by stable atom ID, `neb_end`) are saved in the project. Chained calculations still waiting have no input bundle yet, so `queue.json` keeps their molecules and conditions. Structures read from small-molecule CIF files keep the cell parameters, space group, formula and chosen range in their metadata (`crystal`), and ligand parts (built-in and My parts) can carry η groups (`hapto`) and the metal position (`center`). The xTB executable and the export settings are app settings, not saved in projects. Jobs run with 1.0.0 can be restarted as long as their folder still holds the database. Records left as running by 1.0.0 have no supervisor, so History cannot stop them. Explicitly save conformer sets, energy comparisons and spectrum display settings to separate JSON files from their respective windows. Spectrum display settings are tied to the original result file.

### Verification

The 1.1.0 build, all 16 CTest suites and the Japanese/English documentation site build passed on Linux. Readers and property conventions were checked against small-molecule NWChem 7.3.1 calculations for Raman, ESR, ECD, ORD and ESP/electric-field CUBE output. Restarts were checked with real NWChem 7.3.1 calculations of small molecules too (going on with an “Optimize → Frequencies” stopped by its step limit, going on with a numerical Hessian, a transition-state search with the Hessian of a frequency job, and `reuse` of `freq`). Going on after the window closes and following again were checked with the test process. The build and all 16 CTest suites also passed in the build environment of the AppImage (Ubuntu 24.04, Qt 6.4), and with the AppImage made there a calculation went on after its session ended and was followed and stopped by the next one. Neither has been checked on Windows or macOS. NEB was checked with NWChem 7.3.1 by running the input MolForma generated for HCN → HNC (HF/STO-3G, 5 images, 3 steps) and reading its log (no run was carried to convergence). Chains were checked with the test process, and xTB with a stand-in test program, not with a real xtb. Figures and videos were checked for scale and backgrounds on a Linux display (VTK) and without one (QPainter), GIF with Qt's reader and ImageMagick, and MP4 with ffmpeg 9. NEB, chains, xTB and the figure and video exports have not been checked in the build environment of the AppImage (Qt 6.4). Small-molecule CIF reading was checked with five COD entries (molecules across an inversion center, a formula unit with counterions and lithium, and others), and getting compounds from PubChem with the real service (aspirin with a 3D structure, stearic acid and ferrocene without one, and a name that is not found). Polydentate and η ligands were checked in the GUI by building ferrocene, Fe(tacn)(dien), Ni(porphinato), Ru(terpy)₂ and Co(EDTA), and solvation shells by placing 1,000 waters around trypsin (1,712 atoms) in about a second. These were not checked in the AppImage build environment (Qt 6.4). These small-basis calculations check behavior, not quantitative agreement with experiment. Historical verification records in the OS installation guides refer to the versions stated there.
